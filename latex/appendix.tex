\paragraph{\texttt{datagen.py}} Save it in your project folder and run \texttt{python datagen.py}. It writes six CSV
files into \texttt{data/}. The seed is fixed (42), so every student gets the same files; small differences in results

\lstinputlisting[style=py,caption={\texttt{datagen.py}: the Mboa Fresh Market teaching case.}]{../datagen.py}

\begin{insight}{How the data are built}
Daily demand for product $p$ in store $s$ is
$\mu_{spt}=b_p f_s\, w_{\text{dow}(t)}\, y_{\text{month}(t)}(1+0.0004t)(1+0.35\,\text{promo})$ with an 18\% December uplift,
drawn from a Gamma--Poisson (negative binomial) distribution. Registration waits are exponential with
activity-specific means; 22\% of cases repeat the document check; Monday payments wait 1.8 times longer. Training
is assigned with probability rising in skill, and its true effect is $+3$ points.
\end{insight}

licensed under AGPL-3.0: fine for coursework, but commercial use of software built on it has obligations.
\end{pitfall}

\chapter{Glossary}
\begin{description}[style=nextline,leftmargin=3.6cm,labelwidth=3.4cm,font=\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{navy},itemsep=1pt]
\item[ABC--XYZ] Segmentation by value (ABC) and demand variability (XYZ) to set policies per class.
\item[ADIDA / Croston] Forecasting methods for intermittent demand.
\item[ATE] Average treatment effect, $\E[Y(1)-Y(0)]$.
\item[Bullwhip effect] Amplification of order variability upstream in a supply chain.
\item[Confounder] A variable affecting both treatment and outcome.
\item[Conformal interval] Prediction interval from the empirical quantiles of past out-of-sample errors.
\item[Critical ratio] $c_u/(c_u+c_o)$; the service level that maximises expected newsvendor profit.
\item[CSL / fill rate] Probability of no stock-out per cycle / share of demand served from stock.
\item[DES] Discrete-event simulation.
\item[DiD] Difference-in-differences.
\item[Erlang C] Probability that an arrival waits in an M/M/$c$ queue.
\item[EVPI / VSS] Value of perfect information / value of the stochastic solution.
\item[Event log] Table of case ID, activity and timestamp.
\item[Grain] What one row of a table represents.
\item[Leakage] Using information not available at prediction time.
\item[MASE / WAPE] Scaled and weighted forecast-error metrics.
\item[Phase I / II] Estimating control limits on in-control data / monitoring with them fixed.
\item[Protection interval] Review period plus lead time.
\item[Shadow price] Value of one more unit of a constrained resource.
\item[SLA] Service-level agreement: a measurable promise about waiting.
\item[SRM] Sample-ratio mismatch in an experiment.
\item[Variant] A distinct sequence of activities in an event log.
\end{description}

\chapter{Answers to Selected Exercises}
\begin{description}[style=nextline,leftmargin=1.6cm,labelwidth=1.4cm,font=\sffamily\bfseries\color{navy},itemsep=3pt]
\item[1.3] Performance $=0.72/(0.88\times0.97)=0.843$. Performance (84\%) is the weakest factor: investigate slow running
and minor stops first.
\item[2.2] $L=40\times0.9=36$ patients. To fit 30, $W\le30/40$\,h $=45$ minutes.
\item[3.2] Errors $(2,-2,2,-4)$: MAE 2.5, WAPE $10/10=100\%$, bias $-2/10=-20\%$. MAPE is undefined (division by zero).
\item[4.1] $Q^*=\sqrt{2\cdot11\,000\cdot15\,000/275}\approx1\,095$, $TC^*\approx301\,000$\xaf. At $Q=2\,000$ (holding on the
discounted price) ordering + holding cost is about $352\,000$\xaf, i.e.\ $+51\,000$; the discount saves $0.02\times1\,100\times11\,000=242\,000$\xaf. Accept.
\item[4.3] $\sigma_L=\sqrt{9\cdot36+40^2\cdot4}=82$; SS $=1.96\times82\approx161$ units. Lead-time variability (6\,400 of
6\,724) dominates: negotiate reliable delivery before buying a better forecast.
\item[4.4] $c_u=900$, $c_o=600$, CR $=0.6$, $z=0.253$, $Q^*=85.1$\,kg; expected leftovers $20[z\Phi(z)+\varphi(z)]\approx10.8$\,kg.
If leftovers are discarded, $c_o=1\,500$, CR $=0.375$, $Q^*\approx73.6$\,kg.
\item[6.1] $\rho=0.9$, $L_q=8.1$, $W_q=0.18$\,h $\approx10.8$ min. With 10\% more arrivals $\rho=0.99$ and $W_q\approx2$ hours.
\item[6.3] Two M/M/1 at $\rho=0.8$: $W_q=4$ service times. M/M/2 with $a=1.6$: $C=0.711$, $W_q=0.711/(0.4\mu)\approx1.8$
service times. One shared queue more than halves the wait.
\item[7.2] $n=2(1.96+0.84)^2\cdot0.3\cdot0.7/0.02^2\approx8\,240$ members per arm.
\item[7.4] $\chi^2=(240^2+240^2)/10\,000=11.5$, $p\approx0.0007$: a mismatch. Check assignment code, bot filtering and
logging losses that differ between arms.
\item[Q1] $\sigma_L=4\sqrt5=8.94$, SS $=14.7$; with sd 3, SS $=11.0$; saving $3.7\times500\approx1\,840$\xaf\ a year per item.
\item[Q4] $W=120/30=4$ days; halving $W$ at the same throughput needs WIP of 60 cases.
\end{description}
